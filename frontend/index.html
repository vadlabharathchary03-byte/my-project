SGPA & CGPA Calculation Guide

Malla Reddy Deemed To Be University Academic Evaluation System
SGPA
Semester Grade Point Average

Measures a student's academic performance in a single semester based on registered course credits and grade points earned.

CGPA
Cumulative Grade Point Average

Measures overall cumulative performance across all completed semesters up to the current academic year.

SGPA Formula

How to Calculate SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average)

SGPA =
Σ (Ci × Gi) Σ Ci
=
(C1×G1) + (C2×G2) + ... + (Cn×Gn) C1 + C2 + ... + Cn
Ci = Credits assigned to the ith course (e.g., Theory = 3 or 4, Lab = 1.5 or 2)
Gi = Grade Point (10 to 0) secured in that course
Σ Ci = Total credits registered in the semester
Step-by-Step SGPA Calculation Example:
Subject / Course Credits (C) Letter Grade Grade Point (G) Credit Points (C × G)
Web Technologies 4 O 10 4 × 10 = 40
Data Structures 4 A+ 9 4 × 9 = 36
Operating Systems 3 A 8 3 × 8 = 24
Database Systems 3 B+ 7 3 × 7 = 21
Full-Stack Web Lab 2 O 10 2 × 10 = 20
Total Semester Credits (ΣC) 16 Total Credit Points (Σ[C×G]) 141 Points
Calculation: SGPA = 141 ÷ 16 = 8.81 SGPA (Grade: A+ Distinction)
CGPA Formula

How to Calculate CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average)

CGPA =
Σ (Sj × Cj) Σ Cj
=
(SGPA1×C1) + (SGPA2×C2) + ... + (SGPAm×Cm) C1 + C2 + ... + Cm
Sj = SGPA secured in semester j
Cj = Total credits earned in semester j
Σ Cj = Total credits across all semesters taken so far
Quick CGPA Example: Sem 1 (SGPA 8.5, 20 Credits) • Sem 2 (SGPA 9.0, 20 Credits) → Total Points = (8.5×20) + (9.0×20) = 170 + 180 = 350.
CGPA = 350 ÷ 40 = 8.75 CGPA

Official MRDU 10-Point Academic Grading Scale

Percentage of Marks Letter Grade Grade Point (G) Performance Level
90% – 100% O 10 Outstanding (Distinction)
80% – 89% A+ 9 Excellent
70% – 79% A 8 Very Good
60% – 69% B+ 7 Good
50% – 59% B 6 Above Average
40% – 49% C 5 Pass
Below 40% F 0 Fail / Reappear

Percentage ↔ CGPA Conversion Formula

According to AICTE / University Guidelines:

Equivalent Percentage (%) = (CGPA − 0.75) × 10
Example: If CGPA is 8.5 → (8.5 − 0.75) × 10 = 77.5%
